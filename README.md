# DAOTown - Create DAOs in Minutes!

DAOTown is a DAO management platform that breaks down technical barriers for seamless governance and tokenomics, making decentralized governance accessible to everyone.

**Deployed on Vercel:** [https://dao-town.vercel.app](https://dao-town.vercel.app/)

## How to use the platform

## Register a DAO
There are two ways to create a DAO:

1. By "Creating New DAO Token"
2. or by "Importing Existing DAO Token"
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 34 39 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72b07eb8-01c1-4182-b3be-0406d32ec48a" />


### If you choose "Create New Token" for DAO creation:

a. Enter the DAO's Governance Token details including token symbol, name and total supply to be minted (eg: SHK, Shark and 50000)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 35 27 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32b5c860-c656-42b6-97e2-3c84a9db3b86" />

b. Then enter the DAO Name and a one-liner description about your DAO (eg: Shark's DAO and it's description)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 36 35 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a2779bef-c1ac-4587-8e8b-4bf67fd37b95" />

c. After that, setup DAO's Governance including Joining & Proposal Threshold and Visibility (eg: 5000, 10000 and No, for public DAO) 
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 37 07 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d03a6207-9196-4f25-a6a2-9d2e1fcfaf60" />

d. Once done, you'll be redirected to the explore DAOs page (see the newly created Shark's DAO)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 37 55 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ce7c4729-f364-4d0d-8236-6207d7c819de" />


## If you choose "Import Existing Token" for DAO Creation:

a. Register your DAO first. Enter DAO Name and one-liner description about your DAO (eg: Joker and it's description)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 39 10 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/83e82b20-50fd-485b-8c0a-1e1b024ec7d5" />

b. After that, setup DAO's Governance including Joining & Proposal Threshold and Visibility (eg: 1000, 1000 and No, for public DAO)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 39 20 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/009eb827-3903-4fce-a52e-ae1f50a4c1cb" />

c. Lastly, import your DAO Token by entering it's Contract Address, rest details will be filled automatically
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 39 57 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3d0ce40-0c65-46e5-b99c-8e8b08d7dbd0" />

d. Once done, you'll be redirected to the explore DAOs page (see the newly imported Joker DAO)
<img width="1582" height="956" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-11 at 6 41 34 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1bd80224-295c-4d3f-a875-91207043c385" />

## Register as a User / Member
Fill out the form to register yourself as a user of the DAO Town platform and eventually you can join the DAOs available on the platform.
